Plant your people.
Grow a forest.

Every person you know becomes a tree — friends cluster into groves, close friendships grow tall, drifting ones turn autumn and ask for water. Walk it like a cozy farming game. Remember everyone, forever.

🔒 Your people stay yours. Everything is parsed and stored on your device only — contacts and messages never touch a server. Delete it all anytime.

Your name helps us spot you in your own data (so you don't become a tree in your own forest), and your city plants your home meadow.

Where should your

Pick a seed packet to open it. Start with the 2-minute one — your forest sprouts today; the slow ones just make it richer whenever they arrive.

📇 Export your contacts

  1. Open contacts.google.com (or Apple Contacts → File → Export)
  2. Select all → Export
  3. Pick Google CSV format
  4. Drop the file below 👇

💼 Request LinkedIn
(then forget about it)

  1. LinkedIn → Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data
  2. Choose the larger archive → Request
  3. Email arrives in ~24h — come back and drop Connections.csv here

No file yet? That's the expected path — come back and drop it any time.

📷 Request Instagram
& Messenger

  1. Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Download your information
  2. Some of your information → check Messages + Followers and following
  3. Format JSON · Date range All time → Submit
  4. Repeat for your Facebook account (Messenger)

The zips arrive by email in hours–days. The full app unpacks them here; this prototype can't unzip in-browser yet — request now so they're ready. 📬

💬 On a Mac?
Unlock the magic.

Websites can't read your texts (good!). This helper runs on your Mac, offline — it counts who you text and when, writes one summary file, touches nothing else.

  1. Open Terminal, paste the command, press return
  2. Grant Full Disk Access if asked, run again
  3. Drop the friend-forest-messages.json it makes here
python3 helper/extract_messages.py ~/Desktop/friend-forest-messages.json

The helper lives in this repo's helper/ folder — it counts who you text and when, writes one summary file, and never uploads anything.

🤖 Let Claude write
the field guide

Optional: with your own Anthropic API key, Claude writes a short living profile for your closest friends — who they are, how you probably met, what groves fit them. Skip it and you still get the full forest, just with simpler name cards.

👀 How it works: your browser calls Claude directly with your key — no server in between, and the key is only kept for this session. Cost lands on your key (typically a few cents for ~30 profiles). Get a key at console.anthropic.com.
